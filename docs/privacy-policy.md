# Startby Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Startby plans study and assignment time from your calendar. It is built to keep your information on your device.

## What we collect
Nothing. Startby does not collect, store, or share your personal information on any server. We have no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

## What stays on your device
- Your imported calendar file or feed, your settings, and your edits are stored on your device only.
- Your calendar feed link is stored in your device's Keychain.
- If you allow calendar access, Startby reads your calendar events on your device to avoid scheduling over them, and can add study sessions to a separate "Study Plan" calendar. This information is never sent anywhere.
- If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled on your device.

## Network use
When you add a calendar feed link, Startby downloads that feed directly from the address you provided (for example, your school's learning platform). We do not operate that service and do not receive a copy of the feed.

## Purchases
The optional Pro upgrade is handled entirely by Apple. We do not see or store your payment information.

## Children
Startby does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
