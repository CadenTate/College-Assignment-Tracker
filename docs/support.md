# Startby Support

Need help? Email startbyappsupport@gmail.com. We reply within two business days.

## Common questions

**How do I get my calendar into Startby?**
Import a .ics file from Settings, or paste your school's calendar feed link (in Canvas: Calendar > Calendar Feed). You can also tap "Try sample data" to look around first.

**Why does a task say it cuts into my safety margin?**
Startby plans to finish a few days before the due date. If there isn't enough free time before then, it uses part of that margin and tells you.

**Is my data synced to other devices?**
Not yet. Your data stays on the device where you use Startby.

**How do I restore Pro on a new device?**
Open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchase, using the same Apple ID.
